RRP or above

Partners sell at RRP or above. Monthly RRP excl. VAT: Starter £99, Professional £199, Business £399. Annual is 15% off those figures, matching the public pricing page.

Commission on the net subscription only

40% is on the net subscription. Not call overage. Not telecom pass-through, divert or usage packs.

Lifetime while they pay

Customer plans are 30-day rolling. Your commission is lifetime for as long as that account stays paying.
