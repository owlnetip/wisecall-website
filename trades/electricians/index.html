On the job

Missed calls while you're on a job

Most of your work comes in by phone, and it rarely rings at a good moment. You're up a ladder, under floorboards, or testing a board with the supply isolated. By the time you look, there's a missed call and no voicemail. Some of those calls are the work you want most:

  • A homeowner who wants a quote for a full rewire before they move in.
  • Someone who needs a consumer unit upgrade after an inspection flagged the old board.
  • A new electric car owner looking for a home charger install.
  • A landlord or letting agent with EICRs due on several properties.
  • A caller with no power, an RCD that keeps tripping, or a burning smell from a socket.

That last group doesn't wait. They work down the search results until somebody answers, and whoever answers first usually gets the job.

Urgent faults

Emergency callouts reach you, not voicemail

You decide what counts as urgent. A full loss of power, a burning smell, sparking, or an RCD that won't reset might be on your list. A flickering bulb probably isn't.

When a call matches, WiseCall can transfer it to your mobile or an on-call number straight away, or send you an urgent alert. Everything else is logged for a normal callback. On paid plans, calls can be passed to UK local, national and mobile numbers.

FAQ: electricians' questions, answered

Is an AI receptionist the same as an answering service for electricians?
It does the same front-desk job: answering, taking details and passing them on. It's AI rather than a person, it covers evenings and weekends on every plan, and your allowance counts answered calls, not minutes.
What happens if someone rings with no power or a burning smell?
You decide which faults count as urgent. When a call matches, WiseCall can transfer it to your mobile or on-call number, or send you an urgent alert, straight away. It doesn't diagnose faults or give safety advice.
Will it quote prices for rewires or EV charger installs?
No. Pricing stays with you. It collects what you need to price the job, such as the type of work, the property and the address, then books a visit or a callback.
Can it book quote visits and EICRs into my diary?
Yes, it can book visits and callbacks into your calendar. Which calendar or job software it connects to depends on what you use, and we confirm that during setup.
Can I keep my existing number?
Usually, yes, by porting it to WiseCall. Or use the number included with your plan. We confirm what works for your number during setup.
How much does it cost, and is there a free trial?
Starter is £99 a month on a 30-day rolling plan, with 15% off if you pay annually. The allowance counts AI-answered calls, not minutes. You can try it free on 20 inbound calls, with no card.
