The calls you miss up on the roof

You're on the scaffold or in a loft with no signal, and by lunch there are missed calls with no messages. Those calls tend to be the jobs worth having:

  • A homeowner who wants a price for a new roof before winter.
  • A flat roof over the kitchen that has started to hold water and drip.
  • Gutters that overflow every time it rains, or rotten fascias.
  • A chimney with crumbling pointing or lead flashing that has lifted.
  • A couple planning an extension or loft conversion.
  • Water through a bedroom ceiling the morning after a gale.

After high winds, every roofer nearby gets the same calls, and the homeowner rings down the list until a person answers. That firm usually gets the repair.

Roofing and building FAQ

Does it work for builders as well as roofers?
Yes. You tell it which jobs you take on, such as roof repairs, extensions or loft conversions, and what to ask about each.
What happens when someone rings about a leak or storm damage?
You set the rules for what's urgent. If a call fits, it can ring through to your mobile or on-call number, or alert you at once. It won't diagnose a leak or give safety advice.
Will it give prices for a new roof or an extension?
No, pricing is left to you. It collects the details you'd want before quoting, like the kind of roof or building work and where the property is, then arranges a visit or a callback.
Will it put survey visits in my calendar?
Yes. Which calendar or job software it links to depends on what you already use, and we sort that out with you during onboarding.
Can I keep my current business number?
Usually you can port it across to WiseCall, or use the number that comes with your plan. We check what's possible when you sign up.
How much is it, and can I try it first?
Starter costs £99 a month plus VAT, rolling every 30 days, and drops by 15% if you pay for the year. The allowance is counted in calls the AI answers rather than minutes. The free trial covers 20 inbound calls and doesn't ask for a card.
