FAQ

Pricing Questions

How does WiseCall pricing work?

WiseCall pricing is based on the plan you choose (Starter, Professional or Business) and the number of AI-handled calls, emails, WhatsApp messages, live chat conversations and SMS notifications included each month. Pay monthly on 30-day rolling, or save 15% when you pay annually. All prices exclude VAT.

What is included in every plan?

Every plan includes a 24/7 AI receptionist, call summaries and transcripts, appointment booking, call transfers and routing, and a dashboard. You can try 20 free calls to test your setup, with no card.

What is the contract term?

Plans are 30-day rolling as standard. Cancel before the next month. Or pay annually and save 15%.

What happens if we receive more AI calls than our plan includes?

If your business regularly exceeds its monthly allowance, we will recommend moving to a more suitable plan. Book a demo and we can advise based on your call volume.
